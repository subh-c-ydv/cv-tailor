SHAPE: QUESTION-LED (4 paragraphs)

Paragraph 1 — The question (2-3 sentences): Open with ONE genuine question that the team in this role is probably asking itself, derived from the role's real challenge and phrased in the writer's own words, NOT the JD's phrasing. It must be a real question, not a rhetorical flourish. Then one short sentence on why that question draws the writer in. No flattery of the company and no talk of shared values.

Paragraph 2 — How the writer approaches it (3-4 sentences): The writer's real answer, drawn from experience in CANDIDATE BACKGROUND. Real specifics attributed to the correct employer, including a concrete number or before/after if one exists. State it plainly, as a view formed by doing the work and not as a lecture.

Paragraph 3 — What the writer would bring (2-3 sentences): How this approach could add value to this team and the wider organisation, following the FORWARD-LOOKING RULE. A hypothesis, not a promise.

Paragraph 4 — Close (EXACTLY 1 sentence), following the CLOSING rule.
