SHAPE: DIRECT-AND-SHORT (3 paragraphs)

This shape OVERRIDES the LENGTH guidance: keep the whole letter to 120-170 words. The first reader is probably screening, so make it easy to take in quickly: role fit, seniority and relevant experience should be clear within the first two sentences.

Paragraph 1 — Fit (1-2 sentences): A plain statement of interest in this role and the single most relevant reason the writer fits it.

Paragraph 2 — Evidence and value (2-3 sentences): The one or two strongest pieces of evidence from CANDIDATE BACKGROUND, with at least one concrete number or before/after, attributed to the correct employer. Then one sentence on the value the writer could bring to this team, following the FORWARD-LOOKING RULE.

Paragraph 3 — Close (EXACTLY 1 sentence), following the CLOSING rule.
